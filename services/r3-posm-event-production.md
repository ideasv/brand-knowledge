## Scope

- POSM (Point of Sales Materials): standee, hanger, wobbler, kệ trưng bày
- In-store branding: bảng menu, wall art, window display
- Event branding: booth, backdrop, photo wall
- Activation kit: sampling, demo booth, roadshow
- Brand pop-up store (concept + production direction)
